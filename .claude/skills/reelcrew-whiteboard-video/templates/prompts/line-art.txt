Minimal hand-drawn illustration, pure sketch style, calm doodle look in the manner of a clean notebook drawing. The picture expresses one idea with simple outlines, few lines and lots of empty space; it does not aim at realism. Clean composition, plain background, calm and clear mood; lines, figures and colours consistent with the other scenes of the series.
Background: warm beige paper, exactly #F5EBD7, flat, with no texture.
Lines: dark grey sketch lines of even weight.
Colour: only small conceptual accents in red, orange or blue; no other accent colour, no saturated areas, no gradients.
Figures and objects: simple outlines and a few lines; show relations, change and the core idea, not true proportions, materials or detail.
Layout: wide gaps of empty paper between separate subjects so each can be drawn on its own; subjects do not overlap.
Never: any text, words, letters, numbers, fonts, labels or signs; realism, photographic detail, 3D, painterly texture; complex scenes, dense backgrounds, ornaments, saturated pictures.
